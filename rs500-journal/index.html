Listening notes

Apparently some blues, could be considered country, a little honky-ish

I definitely am stretching myself in some ways, but in others I'm just finding the artists that have influenced ones that are already popular

reading Taleb's "Black Swan," particularly about how herding works and the economics of success in entertainment, how it is a "winner take all" of a tournament, and the winner need not be any better, just lucky - from randomness. He argues that randomness is the great equalizer, because everyone is equally likely to benefit from it, but I don't believe he is entirely correct in that. But the point I'm getting at is that this list is interesting because we will have albums that may or may not have been popular at the time, and may or may not be popular now, but for some reason they are on the list. it most likely has to do with someone saying an album influenced them, then everyone who is influenced by that person citing that influence as well, thus 2 or more degrees removed, by the luck of a few people an album gets undue credit for influencing an artist/genre/movement, when there are multiple albums of the same quality or perhaps even moreso, and of which may have been equally or more influential, which now are on this list. Regardless, I don't know the story of Bonnie Raitt. By the third song I wonder if she is a more legit Carly Simon

Definitely has to be categorized as some light blues with the saxophone going off, but maybe that's just what country did in the 70s like what rock did in the 80s

I like the busy ballad of Nothing Seems to Matter

I really liked "Too Long at the Fair," one that a few years ago I probably would have felt a little uncomfortable with

Listening notes

Is Wilco Country? Is it a person or a band? I don't really know, but I can't help but going in with skepticism

I like this first one, interesting sounds in the background, I don't know what its about, but it is very interesting, dreamy

I don't like songs that end with :5x. I'm probably a little OCD, but just under *Y* minutes bugs me, I'd rather have it be Y:0x minutes long

"Kamera" - is it like how Jenna Maroney says Camera? I like it fine, nice pop song, interesting sounds, and the guitars are very clean - I stopped looking up artists on wikipedia, I want to listen to the albums more unbiased, but sometimes context would probably help.

I gave up not looking them up, it is definitely an interest album. It always surprises me when things came out when I was in high school and I ignored them. I was very skeptical of new music in High School, mostly because the radio was full of pop-punk, of which I was, and still am not a fan. I liked 90s pop alternative like Goo Goo Dolls and Third Eye Blind, but then felt like I grew up past those and went more into classic rock like Boston and Led Zeppelin (having moved past the middle school crush of The Beatles, of which I still did and do love to listen to from time to time, but now I settled into what the other trombone section listened to). There were a lot of indie bands back then that I now listen to a lot that I just didn't know about, and I could have had a very different musical outlook. I probably would have had/needed a different set of friends though

Jesus, etc. comes on with the violins, and bluesy feel, just very interesting.

I don't think my mom would have liked this album, though she never would have heard the words, so she would have probably been ok with the music. I love the line "playing KISS covers beautiful and stoned"

"Pot kettle black" and that last one were great

I guess I find the acoustic simple guitar with all the weird other sounds just a great feel
